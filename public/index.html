    Controls

    Left click / dragSelect units · box-select · click a city (a frame with no units selects your cities)
    Shift + click / dragAdd units or cities to the selection
    Unit icons in the panelClick: select only that unit (or type) · Ctrl + click: remove it from the selection · Shift + click: mark it; when you release Shift, the marked units become the selection
    Double clickOn a unit: all your units of that type on screen · on your city: all your cities on screen
    Right clickMove · attack unit or city · set rally (cities selected: on land for new units, which attack-move there; on water for new ships of coastal cities)
    Alt + right clickCities selected: clear their rally points (both)
    Hold A + right clickUnits selected: attack-move (fight anything on the way; works on the minimap too)
    Shift + right clickUnits selected: queue the move or attack after their current orders; they carry them out one after another. Without Shift, an order replaces the queue
    SUnits selected: stop
    HUnits selected: hold position (fight only what is within reach; any new order ends it)
    DeleteUnits selected: disband (they lose HP until they die; cities can't be disbanded)
    COne land unit selected: make it a Hero for honor (needs the tradition Cult of Heroes), like the Make Hero button
    Q / W / E / R / BBuild infantry / archers / cavalry / knight / transport in every selected city (ships only in coastal cities, marked with an anchor)
    Right click your transportUnits selected: they go on board. The transport must be near a coast; it sails to their shore and they gather there (8 places, only the units that fit go). Your transports light up while units are selected. Right-click more transports with the same selection to share it out; select the units again to send them to another transport instead
    Ctrl + B · Shift + BUnits selected: remove the ships from the selection · keep only the ships
    Ctrl + F · Shift + FUnits selected: remove the full transports from the selection · keep only the full transports
    UTransports selected: unload. Click any land or city: the transport sails to the nearest coast, lands everyone and they march there (they attack an enemy city). Right click or Esc cancels. Quicker: right-click land or an enemy city with the loaded transports selected
    A / S / D / F / GCities selected: build Wall / Tower / Castle / Farm / Barracks in every selected city. A again: Wall II
    Shift + GCities selected: build Barracks paid with honor instead of gold (needs the tradition Brotherhood of Arms)
    Shift + Q / W / E / R / BCities selected: build that unit paid with honor, 2 times its gold cost (needs the tier II tradition Eternal Glory)
    Ctrl + 1–9, then 1–9Save / recall control group (press twice to jump)
    Hold AltShow the attack radius of every city · Alt + drag selects only your cities, not units
    Space · Shift + SpaceCenter on selection · jump to your city captured last (a captured city of yours also pings red on the minimap)
    Drag the minimap's top-right cornerResize the minimap (kept for your next games) · double click the corner: default size
    F2Select all your units
    Arrows · screen edge · middle dragScroll the map
    Mouse wheelZoom
    TTechnologies: Sea (Seafaring: ships, boarding and landing) and Rebellion (what you can do as a liberator)
    O · click the silver starHonor: traditions you learn with the honor your kills earn, such as Heroes
    TabPlayers · F1 this help · Esc deselect

    Capture a city by bringing its HP to zero. Units walk only on land and find their own way around seas; to reach islands such as Britain or Sicily, carry them over in transports. Ships sail only on the sea and are built in coastal cities; they take units on board and land them only on beaches, the sandy stretches of coast (the rest is cliffs). Units regenerate HP after a few seconds out of combat.
