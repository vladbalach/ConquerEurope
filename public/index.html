    Controls

    Left click / dragSelect units · box-select · click a city (a frame with no units selects your cities)
    Shift + click / dragAdd units or cities to the selection
    Unit icons in the panelClick: select only that unit (or type) · Ctrl + click: remove it from the selection · Shift + click: mark it; when you release Shift, the marked units become the selection
    Double clickOn a unit: all your units of that type on screen · on your city: all your cities on screen
    Right clickMove · attack unit or city · set rally (city selected; new units attack-move there)
    Alt + right clickCities selected: clear their rally points
    Hold A + right clickUnits selected: attack-move (fight anything on the way; works on the minimap too)
    SUnits selected: stop
    HUnits selected: hold position (fight only what is within reach; any new order ends it)
    DeleteUnits selected: disband (they lose HP until they die; cities can't be disbanded)
    Q / W / E / RBuild infantry / archers / cavalry / knight in every selected city
    A / S / D / F / GCities selected: build Wall / Tower / Castle / Farm / Barracks in every selected city
    Ctrl + 1–9, then 1–9Save / recall control group (press twice to jump)
    Hold AltShow the attack radius of every city · Alt + drag selects only your cities, not units
    SpaceCenter on selection
    F2Select all your units
    Arrows · screen edge · middle dragScroll the map
    Mouse wheelZoom
    TTechnologies (the Rebellion tab: what you can do as a liberator)
    TabPlayers · F1 this help · Esc deselect
